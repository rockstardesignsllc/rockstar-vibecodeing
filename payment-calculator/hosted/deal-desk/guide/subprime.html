Start here

Quick start: your first quote in 5 minutes

Do these steps in order. Each one is explained in detail later in this guide.

  1. Open your dealer page: https://your-site/home. Bookmark it.
  2. Type your name, your cell number and your email in the Salesperson section. You only do this once per computer or phone.
  3. Type the customer's first and last name. Both are required. Add their phone, email, address, state and ZIP code.
  4. Type the vehicle: stock number, year, make, model and trim.
  5. Type the purchase price, the fees, and the down payment. Sales tax fills in by itself from the customer's state and ZIP.
  6. If they have a trade, type what it is worth and what they still owe on it.
  7. Look at the black box on the right. That is the payment. Check it makes sense.
  8. Press Copy customer link. Paste it in a text or email to the customer. Done.
Tip

Want to sit with the customer instead? Press Copy desking link and open it on your screen. See "Work a deal at the desk".

Do this once

One-time setup

Dealership

Your dealership name, logo and colors are already set up by Rockstar. They show as "(set in code settings)" and you cannot change them here. If something is wrong, contact Rockstar support.

Salesperson

  1. Salesperson name: your name. The customer sees it on their quote.
  2. Salesperson cell: your cell number. This adds a Text button to the customer's quote so they can text you in one tap.
  3. Salesperson notification email: where your quote alerts and "I want this deal" emails go. Use an email you check.
Tip

These fields are saved on this computer or phone as you type. You will not need to type them again on this device. Using a different device? Type them once there too.

Shared computer

Sharing a computer with another salesperson? Click Forget saved info at the top of the Dealership section, then type your own details. Otherwise your customers will get the other person's name and number.

Build a quote

Build a quote, section by section

Work from top to bottom. The payment on the right updates as you type.

Step 1. Customer

  1. Type the First name and Last name. These are required. Without them the link will not copy and the quote cannot be found later.
  2. Type the Email and Phone.
  3. Type the Street address, City, State and ZIP code. Use the 2-letter state, like TN.
Why state and ZIP matter

The State and ZIP code are what the sales tax is worked out from. Use the customer's home address, not the dealership's. An out-of-state buyer gets their own state's tax.

Step 2. Vehicle

Type the stock number, VIN (17 characters), year, make, model and trim, and the Odometer (miles).

Step 3. Pricing

Enter whole dollar amounts. Do not type "$" or commas.

  1. Purchase price: the selling price. Whole dollars only, no commas or $ sign.
  2. Total sales tax in dollars: fills in by itself (see the next page). Only type here if you switched to "Enter tax by hand".
  3. Title, registration and dealer fees in dollars: all of those fees added together, in dollars.
  4. Of those fees, the dealer doc fee: how much of the fees above is your doc fee. It is used only to work out the tax, because some states tax the doc fee.
  5. Vehicle is: choose New or Used. Some states treat trade-ins differently on new vehicles.
  6. Starting down payment: the down payment the quote opens with. The customer can change it.
  7. Rebate: any rebate in dollars. Leave it blank if there is none.

Automatic sales tax

The sales tax works itself out from the customer's state and ZIP code. It uses that state's vehicle tax rules, local tax rates, trade-in credit, and doc fee rules. The gray box under the tax field shows the math, so you can check it.

  1. Fill in the customer's State and ZIP code first.
  2. Fill in the price, fees, doc fee, New or Used, and the trade.
  3. Read the tax box. The top line is the tax amount. The lines below show how it was worked out.
  4. Confirm the tax before you sign any contract. It is an estimate.

Typing the tax yourself

Click Enter tax by hand to turn the automatic tax off and type the total tax in dollars. Click Calculate tax from customer ZIP to turn it back on. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Dollars, not percent

Type the tax in dollars, not a percentage. If you type 7 for 7%, the page warns you: "That looks like a percentage."

If the tax does not load

If you see "Tax rates could not load", refresh the page. If it keeps happening, use Enter tax by hand and tell Rockstar support.

Step 4. Trade

No trade? Leave the amounts at 0 and the vehicle fields blank.

  1. Trade allowance: what you are giving the customer for their trade.
  2. Trade balance owed: what the customer still owes on it (their payoff).
  3. Read the colored line under the amounts. Green means positive equity, which lowers the amount financed. If they owe more than it is worth, it shows negative equity, which is added to the amount financed.
  4. Fill in the trade year, make, model, trim, VIN and odometer.

Setting up the grid

  1. Click Multi-Payment grid.
  2. In Interest rate, choose Fixed rate (shown) or Hidden rate, and type the APR.
  3. In Payment grid, pick Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3.
  4. Type Down payment 1, 2 and 3.
  5. Check the grid on the right. The highlighted payment is the one the customer starts on.
Tip

If a down payment is bigger than the amount being financed, the page caps it and tells you. Lower that down payment.

Tip

Your layout, grid terms and grid down payments are remembered on this device.

Set the interest rate

Sub-prime quotes have two choices. Hidden rate is on by default.

ChoiceWhat the customer seesWhen to use it
Hidden rateThe payment only. No rate is shown anywhere on the quote.Most sub-prime deals. The rate is used to work out the payment but stays private.
Fixed rateThe rate, which they cannot change.When you have an approval and want to show the rate.
  1. Click Hidden rate or Fixed rate.
  2. Type the APR, for example 18.99. It is the rate used for the payment.
Built in for sub-prime

Every sub-prime quote says: "All payments are subject to lender approval." There is no credit score slider and no lease on sub-prime quotes.

Set the terms

With the Adjustable layout, you choose which terms the customer can pick from. Terms run 24 to 84 months (24, 36, 48, 60, 72 and 84).

  1. Check the box on every term the customer may choose. Uncheck the ones you do not want to offer.
  2. Want to lock the term? Check only one box. The customer then sees "Term set by Your dealership" and cannot change it.
  3. Pick the Starting term, the term the quote opens on.
Send it

Send the quote

The black box on the right of the dealer page shows the payment and the deal math. Below it are your buttons.

Copy customer linkCopies the link for the customer. Paste it into a text or email. This is the one you will use most.
Copy desking linkCopies a link for you to open at the desk with the customer. See "Work a deal at the desk".
Print this offerPrints a worksheet with every grid payment for the customer to circle and sign. Only shows with the Multi-Payment grid.
Preview customer viewOpens the customer's page in a new tab so you can check it. It sends no alerts.
  1. Check the payment and the numbers in the black box.
  2. Click Preview customer view and look it over.
  3. Click Copy customer link. The button says "Copied".
  4. Open your texting app or email, paste the link, and send it.
Link will not copy?

If the button will not copy and you see "Enter the customer's first and last name first", fill in both names at the top.

Tip

Each new version of a deal gets its own quote ID (shown under the buttons). Changed the numbers? Copy and send a new link. The old link still shows the old numbers.

Printing the offer (grid layout only)

  1. Choose the Multi-Payment grid layout.
  2. Click Print this offer.
  3. Print it, or choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
  4. Page 1 has the customer, vehicle and pricing. Page 2 has all 9 payments, with "Circle the payment that works best for you", blank lines for the down payment, term and payment they choose, and a signature box.

What sends an alert

Copying a link or printing sends the quote to your dealership's system and saves it to Saved quotes. Preview and desking views never send alerts.

Your customer's side

What your customer sees

The link opens a page with your dealership's logo and colors. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

What the customer can do

  • Slide the target monthly payment. The down payment changes to match.
  • Change the down payment with the slider or by typing it.
  • Tap a term to choose it (if you offered more than one).
  • Pick a payment from the grid (grid layout only).
  • Lock in this payment: tells you they want this deal.
  • Text this deal to my salesperson: texts you the deal. Shows only on phones and tablets, and only if you entered your cell.
  • Save this offer as a PDF: saves or prints their offer.

Further down, the customer sees their deal details, the full deal breakdown (price, tax, fees, trade, down payment, amount financed, rate and term), your name and number, and the financing disclosure.

Edited links are flagged

If anyone changes the numbers inside a link, the page shows: "Heads up: this offer link was changed after it was sent." Always confirm the numbers before you contract.

When a customer says yes

When the customer taps Lock in this payment:

  1. Their email app opens with a message to your notification email that says which deal they want. They press Send.
  2. You get a "locked in" alert, and the quote shows as Locked in payment in Saved quotes.
  3. If their email app did not open, the page offers Copy my offer, Text you, or Email the dealership.

What you do next

  1. Call or text the customer right away. Speed wins the deal.
  2. Open the quote from Saved quotes to see exactly what they picked.
  3. Confirm credit, the rate, tax, fees and rebates before you write the contract.

Work a deal at the desk

The desking link opens the customer's page for you to work at the desk with the customer sitting next to you.

  1. Build the quote on your dealer page.
  2. Click Copy desking link.
  3. Paste it into your browser's address bar and press Enter. Turn the screen toward the customer.
  4. Move the sliders together until the payment works for them.
  5. Click Save this offer as a PDF to print it. The desking printout has a "Lock in Your Deal" box for the customer to print their name, sign and date.
Tip

The desking view hides the Lock in and Text buttons, because you are right there with the customer. Opening it does not send a "quote opened" alert.

Find old quotes (Saved quotes)

Every quote your team sent, printed or got locked in is saved for 90 days.

Sign in

  1. Go to https://your-site/saved-quotes, or click Saved quotes at the top of your dealer page.
  2. Type your dealership's access code (it looks like XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). If asked, also type the dealership name exactly as it shows on your quotes.
  3. Click Show my quotes. This device stays signed in until you click Sign out.

Use the list

  1. Newest quotes are at the top. Each row shows the customer, vehicle, payment, status, salesperson and date.
  2. Type in the search box to find a quote by name, phone, vehicle, stock number or salesperson.
  3. Click Open quote to see the full offer and pick the deal back up.
  4. Click Refresh to load new quotes.

FAQ

Do I need to install anything?
No. It all runs in your web browser on any computer, phone or tablet.
Why did my name and number disappear?
Your details are saved on each device. On a new device, or after clearing your browser, type them once more.
Is the sales tax exact?
It is an estimate from the customer's state rules and ZIP code tax tables. Always confirm the tax before you contract.
The customer lives in another state. What tax do I use?
Type their home state and ZIP. The calculator uses their state's rules.
Why is there no credit score slider or lease?
Sub-prime quotes leave them out on purpose. Use Hidden or Fixed rate.
Can the customer change the price?
No. They can only change the payment, down payment, term within what you allowed.
Why is there no Text button on the customer's quote?
It only shows on phones and tablets, and only if you typed your salesperson cell number.
Why is there no Print this offer button?
It only shows with the Multi-Payment grid layout. With Adjustable, use Save this offer as a PDF on the customer page.
I changed the deal. Does the old link update?
No. Each version gets its own quote ID. Send the new link.
How long are quotes kept?
90 days after they were last saved.
Where do the alerts go?
To your dealership's system as set up by Rockstar, and the customer's "lock in" email goes to the salesperson notification email.

Glossary

Amount financed
What the customer borrows: total price, minus trade equity, rebate and down payment.
APR
Annual percentage rate. The yearly interest rate on the loan.
Customer link
The link you send the customer. Opens their personal quote.
Desking link
A link for you to open at the desk with the customer. No Lock in button, no alerts.
Doc fee
Your dealer documentation fee. Some states tax it.
Down payment
Cash the customer puts down.
Fixed rate
A rate you set that the customer can see but not change.
Hidden rate
A rate used for the payment but never shown to the customer.
Lock in
The customer tapping the button that says they want the deal.
Multi-Payment grid
A table of 9 payments: 3 terms by 3 down payments.
Negative equity
When the customer owes more on the trade than it is worth. Added to the amount financed.
Quote ID
A short code for one version of a deal, like Q1A2B3C4.
Rebate
Money off from the manufacturer or dealer.
Sub-prime
Financing for customers with lower or rebuilding credit. Payments are subject to lender approval.
Term
How many months the loan lasts.
Trade allowance
What you give the customer for their trade.
Trade balance owed
What the customer still owes on their trade (the payoff).
Trade equity
Trade allowance minus balance owed. Positive equity lowers the amount financed.
