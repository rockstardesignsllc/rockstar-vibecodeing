Straight answers

Questions contractors ask.

Can it handle emergency calls?

Yes. You decide what counts as an emergency. RockstarAI can walk the caller through quick safety steps, book the first slot or transfer straight to whoever's on call.

Will it quote prices?

Only what you approve, like your service call fee or starting prices. Anything custom gets handed to you with notes.

Does it know my service area?

Yes. We load your service area, so it books the jobs you want and politely turns away the ones you don't.

Can it work with my scheduling software?

We connect it to the calendar and tools you use during setup, so jobs land where you already look.

Can it ask for Google reviews?

Yes. RockstarAI Follow Up sends your review link after the job, so happy customers turn into 5 star reviews.
