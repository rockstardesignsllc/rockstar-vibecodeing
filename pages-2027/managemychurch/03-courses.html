Straight answers

Questions about Courses.

Do we need fancy video equipment?

Not at all. A phone and a quiet room work great. Your people want to hear from you, not a studio.

Can we use teaching we already have?

Yes. Recorded sermons, classes and studies can all become courses.

Can we see who's taking courses?

Yes. You'll see who's enrolled, how far they've gotten and who's finished.

Will you help us build the first one?

Yes. We'll help you set up your first course so you can see how easy it is.
