Why it matters

Nobody should miss the message because they can't hear it or don't speak the language.

Every Sunday, someone in your pews is straining to hear, or hearing words they don't fully understand. Church Captions puts the sermon right in their hands, so they can take it all in with everyone else.

For the hearing impaired

People scan a QR code and read what your pastor is saying, word for word, on their phone in real time. No special equipment, no front row seat required.

For those who speak another language

The same QR code lets people pick from 13 languages and see the message translated live on their phone or tablet. They can even put in headphones and listen in their native language.

Straight answers

Questions pastors ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Church Captions is 100% free for churches in need. We do ask that you pay it forward with a donation, because that's what covers the cost of keeping it running and improving it.

Who can use Church Captions?

Christian churches only. It was built to help Christian churches reach everyone in the room with the message.

How fast can we get started?

Right away. It's 100% self serve, so once you sign up you can set it up instantly and start using it.

Do people need to download an app?

No. They scan your church's QR code with their phone or tablet and the live captions open right up.

How many languages does it translate?

13 languages. People pick their language from the same QR code and see the message translated in real time. They can also put in headphones and tap the audio icon to listen in their language.

How accurate is the translation?

Translation is provided by Google Translate. It's very helpful, but it isn't always 100% accurate, so some words or phrases may come through a little differently than your pastor said them.

Can we show captions on our screens?

Yes. You can use Church Captions as an overlay in ProPresenter for live transcription on the big screen while your pastor preaches.
