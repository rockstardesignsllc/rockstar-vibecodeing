# Mat Koenig (matkoenig.com)

> Mat Koenig is a business leader, pastor and speaker. He founded Rockstar Designs LLC, home of RockstarAI, and pastors Church. (Local Church Gathering) in Smithville, Tennessee with his wife.

30 years in sales, training and digital marketing. National sales trainer. NADA, Digital Dealer and NPDA speaker. Founder of the Rockstar Auto Conference. Speaks on AI, sales training and leadership for business events, and preaches and teaches at church and ministry events.
