Straight answers

Questions about RockstarAI Call Coach.

Which calls get scored?

Inbound sales and service calls longer than a minute. Voicemails, wrong numbers and calls answered by AI are skipped. Scoring outbound calls is available as an add on.

Do I need my own phone script?

No. If you have one, every call is scored against it. If you don't, our built in scorecard grades the basics: greeting, discovery, handling questions and asking for the appointment.

Who sees the scores?

The rep who took the call and your managers. The dashboard is for your management team only.

What phone system does it work with?

Calls that run through your Rockstar phone system. Not on it yet? We'll get you set up.

How is it different from call tracking tools like CallRevu or Calldrip?

Most call tools hand managers a pile of recordings and a score. Call Coach turns every call into coaching the rep reads within minutes, in the words of your own script.

How is it priced?

Monthly, based on how many calls you want scored. Book a demo and we'll quote it on your real call volume.
