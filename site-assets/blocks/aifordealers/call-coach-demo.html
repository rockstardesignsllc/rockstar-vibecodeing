What happens when you call

Three minutes. Three steps. Real coaching.

Pick your call

Rex answers and asks what you want to practice: sales, BDC or service. Then he asks for the situation you want to run and how tough the customer should be: easy, medium or hard.

Rex plays the customer

About two minutes of role-play. Rex is the shopper on the other end. You handle the call the way you would on any day at the store.

Get coached

Rex tells you one thing you nailed and one thing to tune up, right on the call. Your full scorecard follows by email about 15 minutes later.

For the demo, Rex coaches against our sample phone frameworks for sales, BDC and service. If he suggests wording your store doesn't use, that's expected. The Rex we build for you coaches to your scripts, not ours.
