A SIMPLE FOUNDATION

We are not trying to impress people with polished religion.

We want to be people who trust God’s Word, follow Jesus, love one another, and make disciples. These beliefs are not meant to create distance. They are meant to bring clarity.

CORE BELIEFS

Here is what we believe.

About God

We believe there is one true God who is holy, loving, just, and eternal. He is the Creator of all things and worthy of all worship.

About Jesus

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man. He lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and is the only way to salvation.

About the Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts, comforts, empowers, and leads believers. He helps us live in obedience to God and grow in Christlikeness.

About the Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority for truth, faith, and daily living.

About Humanity

We believe every person is made in the image of God and has deep value. We also believe all people have sinned and need the forgiveness and rescue found only in Jesus.

About Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. It cannot be earned by works or religious effort.

About the Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, love one another, grow in truth, and make disciples.

About Discipleship

We believe every believer is called to follow Jesus daily, be transformed by His Spirit, and help others do the same.

About Eternity

We believe Jesus will return, the dead will be raised, and every person will spend eternity either with God or separated from Him.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Beliefs are not just statements. They shape real life.

What you believe about God, truth, sin, grace, and Jesus will affect how you suffer, how you love, how you repent, and how you live. Good theology should lead to faithful living.
