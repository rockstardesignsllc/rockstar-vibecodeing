WHAT TO EXPECT

What a Sunday with us looks like.

Our gathering feels less like a stiff service and more like family — relaxed, friendly, and easy to just show up to. Here's what to expect when you join us.

Kids stay with us

We keep the kids with us in the gathering. Our Kids Team hangs out and colors with the little ones in the same space, so mom and dad are free to listen and worship — and kids get to see their parents drawing close to God.

About an hour

Our Sunday gathering is typically about an hour, so it's easy to fit into your morning.

Come as you are

Come however you're dressed, with whatever you have. Everyone is welcome here — Jesus isn't impressed or upset by the clothes we wear.

Friendly faces

Everyone here is pretty welcoming, so come ready to make a few new friends.
