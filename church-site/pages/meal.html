WHY WE DO THIS

Scripture is full of people gathered around tables.

From the feeding of the five thousand, to the Last Supper, to Jesus cooking breakfast on the beach for His disciples after the resurrection — God has always used meals as a place of connection, healing, and real presence.

We believe some of the best conversations, deepest friendships, and most honest moments still happen across the table. So every month, on the last Sunday, we practice the simple and beautiful habit of breaking bread together.

This meal is made possible by the generosity of our whole community. If you can bring something, we would love that. If you can’t, we still want you to come and eat with us — because there will be plenty of food for everyone.

“And day by day, attending the temple together and breaking bread in their homes, they received their food with glad and generous hearts.”

Acts 2:46
