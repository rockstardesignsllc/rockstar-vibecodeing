Optional. Paste the text or upload a PDF and it is read in for you. A sales related call is scored on the Sales (or BDC) framework and a service related call on the Service framework. Any call with no matching framework, and every call when these are all blank, is scored on the built-in General scorecard: listening, ownership, next steps and how satisfied the customer sounded.

Scores a single call right now, even an old one. Paste the conversation ID from the end of the HighLevel conversation URL (it scores that conversation's longest answered inbound call), or a call's message ID. Use a message ID to test an outbound call.
