Start here

This guide walks you through turning on Call Coach for one dealership. Follow the parts in order and do not skip the test.

What Call Coach does: it reads the dealership's recorded phone calls, scores each one, and sends coaching to the salesperson and their managers. Once a dealer is set up, it runs by itself. Nobody clicks anything day to day.

The setup page (one page for every dealer)
https://rockstar-ai-audit.rockstar-designs-llc.workers.dev/coach/setup
The dealer never sees this page. Only Rockstar staff use it.

Part 1: Gather these first 5 minutes

  1. The admin key. Get it from Mat. Never share it with a dealer and never paste it into a chat or email.
  2. The dealer's phone scripts, if they have them. Sales, BDC, Service. Text or PDF both work. No scripts is fine: calls are then scored on the built-in General scorecard (listening, ownership, next steps, customer satisfaction). If they bought the outbound add-on, get their outbound script too.
  3. Manager emails. The people who should be copied on every coaching email and get the 7 AM daily summary.
  4. Check their users. In the dealer's account, go to Settings, My Staff. Every person who answers phones needs to be a user with their real first name and a working email. This is how the coaching email finds the right person.

Part 2: Create the token 5 minutes

The token is a password that lets Call Coach read that one dealer's calls. You create it inside the dealer's own account.

  1. Log in to Rockstar and switch into the dealer's sub-account. Check the dealer's name at the top left. A token made in the wrong account will be rejected.
  2. Go to Settings, then Private Integrations, then Create new integration.
  3. Name it Rockstar Call Coach.
  4. Check exactly these seven permissions:
    • View Conversations
    • View Conversation Messages
    • Edit Conversation Messages
    • View Contacts
    • Edit Contacts
    • View Users
    • View Locations
  5. Click Create and copy the token right away. It is only shown once. If you lose it, delete the integration and make a new one.
  6. Copy the sub-account ID. It is the code in the browser address right after /location/.

Part 3: Fill in the setup page 5 to 10 minutes

  1. Open the setup page and type the admin key.
  2. Paste the sub-account ID. Paste the token.
  3. Name: leave it blank. It fills in the dealership's name automatically.
  4. Time zone: leave it blank unless the 7 AM summary should use a different time zone than the account's.
  5. Manager emails: type them separated by commas.
  6. Phone numbers by department: leave it blank. The AI sorts every call by what the customer called about.
  7. Call frameworks: paste each script the dealer gave you into its box, or click Choose File to upload the PDF and wait for the text to appear. Read it over once. Leave a box blank if there is no script for it.
  8. Outbound add-on only: check "Also score outbound calls" and fill in the Outbound box.
  9. Uncheck "Scoring is on" for now. You will turn it on after the test.
  10. Click Save settings and wait for the green "Saved" message. A red message tells you what to fix.

Part 4: Test it 5 to 10 minutes

  1. In the dealer's account, open Conversations and find a real inbound sales call that lasted at least two minutes and was answered by a person.
  2. Copy the conversation ID. It is the code at the very end of the browser address.
  3. On the setup page, paste it into "Test on one call". Leave "Also write the contact note and send the email" unchecked. Click Score this call and wait up to a minute.
  4. Click Open the full summary and check all four:
    • The dealership's name is correct at the top.
    • The score and coaching look fair for that call.
    • The recording plays.
    • "Open this call in Rockstar" takes you to the right conversation.
  5. Go back and score the same call again with the box checked. This one is real. Then confirm:
    • A note appeared on that customer's contact.
    • The coaching email arrived for the managers.
If any check fails, stop. Tell Mat before going live.

Part 5: Go live 3 minutes

  1. On the setup page, check "Scoring is on" and click Save settings.
  2. From this moment, every new inbound call over 60 seconds is scored about 10 to 15 minutes after it ends. Calls from before you turned it on are left alone.
  3. Click My dealers at the top of the setup page. Find the dealer and click Copy link in the Dashboard link column.
  4. Add the dashboard to the dealer's menu: in the agency view go to Settings, Custom Menu Links, and create a new link. Title it Call Coach, paste the link, show it only in this dealer's sub-account, and set it to open inside the app.
  5. Log in to the dealer's account and click Call Coach in the menu to confirm the dashboard opens.

What the dealer gets every day

  • A topic on every call: Sales related, Service related or Other. Sales calls are scored on the sales script, service calls on the service script, and anything without a matching script on the General scorecard.
  • A note on the customer's contact for each scored call, with the score, summary, promised follow-ups, what went well, tune-ups and links.
  • An email to the salesperson and managers: "Call Scoring for [customer] [phone] complete at [date and time]" with a button to the coaching page.
  • A 7 AM summary to managers of yesterday's calls, grouped by salesperson, with color-coded average scores. It opens with frustrated customers and promised follow-ups that need attention.
  • The Call Coach dashboard in their menu. It shows the last 30 days by staff member. Click a name to see all of that person's calls.

Managing dealers later

Click My dealers on the setup page to see every dealer that is set up.

  • Edit: loads that dealer's settings into the page. Change what you need and click Save settings. Leave the token box blank to keep the saved token.
  • Pause: click Edit, uncheck "Scoring is on", Save. Everything is kept and you can turn it back on later.
  • Stop emails only: click Edit, uncheck "Send coaching emails and the 7 AM summary", Save. Notes and the dashboard keep working.
  • Delete (a dealer cancels): click Delete and confirm. This erases their settings and scored calls from Call Coach for good. Notes already written on their contacts stay. Also remove their Call Coach menu link and delete the Rockstar Call Coach integration in their account.
