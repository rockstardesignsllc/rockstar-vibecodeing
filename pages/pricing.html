What Every Proposal Includes

What's in every RockstarAI proposal.

No line items hidden in an appendix. Every proposal includes each of these, broken out by product.

Setup and configuration

One-time cost for configuring the AI with your inventory, staff, hours, and process rules. Includes the review and approval session before anything goes live.

Monthly platform fee

The recurring cost per product — AI Sales, AI BDC, AI Receptionist — per month. This covers the AI platform, your dedicated phone number (where applicable), and standard support.

Usage and volume

Some products include a usage component based on call minutes, message volume, or active conversations. Usage rates are itemized in the proposal, not buried in terms.

Integration costs

CRM connections, DMS integrations, and lead source connectors are priced separately and itemized. You'll see exactly what each integration costs before you sign.

Onboarding support

Included in setup: the onboarding session, inventory sync, workflow approval, and launch check. Ongoing configuration changes after launch are handled through standard support.

Contract terms

Contract length and cancellation terms are included in every proposal. You'll know the commitment before you sign — not after you've already onboarded.

What Affects Your Scope

What changes the price for your store.

These are the variables that affect your specific proposal. The audit review confirms each one before we scope your package.

Which products you need

AI Sales, AI BDC, and AI Receptionist are priced individually. Some stores need all three. Some start with one. The audit helps identify where to start based on where you're currently losing the most opportunities.

Your lead volume

Higher lead volume affects usage-based components. We review your current monthly lead count during the audit so usage estimates in your proposal are accurate — not generic averages.

Your call volume

AI Receptionist pricing includes a call minute estimate based on your inbound call volume. Reviewing your current phone traffic during the audit means the estimate in your proposal reflects your actual store — not a placeholder.

CRM and integration requirements

Some CRMs connect via standard integration with no additional cost. Others require custom connections that are scoped and priced separately. Your proposal lists every integration and its associated cost.

Your dealership type

Automotive, powersports, RV, and marine dealerships are all supported. The configuration complexity differs by inventory type, which affects setup time and cost for more specialized product lines.

Number of locations

Multi-location dealerships are scoped differently than single-store operations. Each location's inventory, staff, and hours are configured independently. Multi-location pricing is available and outlined in your proposal.

Ask Every Vendor This

Questions to ask before you sign any AI contract.

  • Is the setup fee one-time or recurring? What does it include?
  • Are there usage charges on top of the monthly fee?
  • Which integrations are included and which cost extra?
  • What is the full contract length and cancellation policy?
  • What happens to my data and phone number if I cancel?
  • Does pricing change after the initial term? If so, by how much?
  • Are there per-seat or per-user fees in addition to the platform fee?
  • What support is included and what is billed separately?
  • Is onboarding billed separately from setup?
  • Are configuration changes after launch included or billed as professional services?
  • Are there volume caps on messages, calls, or conversations?
  • What triggers an overage charge and how is it billed?
