What It Costs

RockstarAI pricing is scoped to your dealership.

We don't publish a one-size price because a single-point dealership has different needs than a rooftop group. Your proposal is itemized and built around the agents you actually need.

What's in every proposal

  • One-time setup and configuration cost
  • Monthly platform and support cost
  • Usage-based costs if any (calls, texts, AI tokens)
  • Integration and connection fees
  • Contract length and renewal terms
  • What you're responsible for providing

What affects your scope

  • Which agents: Sales, BDC, Receptionist, or all three
  • Channels: phone, text, web chat, or combined
  • Volume: inbound calls and leads per month
  • Integrations: CRM, DMS, scheduling, inventory
  • Number of locations
  • Required configuration testing and launch support

Before You Compare

Ask every vendor the same questions.

Price alone doesn't tell you what you'll pay twelve months in. When reviewing any dealership AI proposal, request the following:

  • Total cost over the full contract term, not just monthly
  • What triggers an overage or usage charge
  • Which integrations are included vs billed separately
  • What the onboarding and setup process requires from your team
  • Cancellation terms and data ownership at the end of the contract
  • Response time for support issues

We apply these same questions to ourselves. Your audit proposal will answer each one.
