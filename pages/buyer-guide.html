Dealership AI Buyer Guide

What is the best AI for a car dealership?

The honest answer: it depends on what your store actually needs. This guide walks through how to evaluate AI for lead response, phone coverage, follow-up, and appointment booking — before you sign anything.

The Right Question

Most dealers are asking the wrong question.

"What AI should I buy?" is less useful than "What problem am I solving?" Define the job first — then find the tool that does it.

Leads going unanswered?

If internet leads sit for more than 5 minutes before a human reaches them, the problem is lead response. You need an AI that fires immediately and holds the conversation until a salesperson is available.

Phones going to voicemail?

If customers call and no one picks up — especially nights and weekends — the problem is phone coverage. You need an AI receptionist that answers live, answers questions, and books appointments.

Staff drowning in follow-up?

If your BDC or salespeople spend hours on routine follow-up texts and calls, the problem is capacity. You need an AI BDC that handles the volume and hands off only qualified opportunities.

What to Look For

Five things that separate the real products from the demos.

01

It's configured for your store

The best dealership AI is not a generic chatbot. It knows your inventory, your hours, your staff names, and your approved responses. Ask every vendor how their product is specifically configured for your store — and how long that takes.

02

It works across the right channels

Your customers text, call, and chat. An AI that only handles one channel leaves gaps. Confirm exactly which channels are covered in the proposal you receive, and which require additional modules or cost.

03

It can be tested before you commit

Any vendor worth considering will let you call a demo number and submit a test lead before you sign. If they won't, that tells you something. Test it the way your customers would use it — with a real question about a real vehicle.

04

The pricing is itemized

Require an itemized proposal: setup fee, monthly platform fee, usage-based charges, and per-integration costs. "Starts at" numbers without clear scope are a negotiating tactic, not a real proposal.

05

The contract has a clear exit

Ask about the contract length and what happens to your data, your phone numbers, and your leads if you leave. A 2-year lock-in on unproven software is a significant risk. Get cancellation terms in writing before signing.

06

You own the process definition

Your AI should follow your dealership's process — not a vendor's best-practice template. The vendor should document what the AI says and does, and your team should approve it before anything goes live.

What the Category Covers

The dealership AI landscape — briefly.

Several companies offer AI for car dealerships. They are not interchangeable. The categories below reflect different product philosophies.

Full-platform solutions

Large platforms (like Impel) cover the full customer lifecycle — from inventory merchandising to post-purchase follow-up. They include many modules. Confirm which are in your proposed package and whether you'll actually use all of them.

Communication-focused tools

Tools like Numa focus on business texting, missed call handling, and service communication. Evaluate whether their strength in one area matches the specific channel where your dealership is losing leads.

Voice AI specialists

STELLA and similar products focus on AI voice handling for inbound calls. If your primary problem is unanswered phone calls, a voice-first product may fit. Verify what it can actually do with a sales question during a live call.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Bring this list to your next proposal meeting.

  • What exactly does your AI do when a lead comes in at 10pm on a Saturday?
  • How long does onboarding take and what does my team need to provide?
  • Can I hear the actual voice your AI uses on my dealership's phone calls?
  • Which CRM and DMS integrations are included in the base price?
  • What happens to my leads and phone number if I leave the platform?
  • How is the AI configured for my specific inventory and store hours?
  • What does your AI say when it doesn't know the answer to a question?
  • How does handoff to a human work and what triggers it?
  • Can I test the product with a real lead before signing the contract?
  • What is the full contract length and what are the cancellation terms?
  • What metrics will I see in my reporting dashboard and how often?
  • Do you serve powersports dealers — motorcycle, ATV, RV, marine?
