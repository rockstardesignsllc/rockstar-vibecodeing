Test the difficult situations

Ask about a vehicle that is no longer available. Ask for a person who does not answer. Request an appointment outside store hours. Change the vehicle you want halfway through the conversation. Check that the answer, booking, and staff notification all make sense.

Make the decision on dealership results

Use the same evaluation period and scoring method for each provider. Measure response quality, completed handoffs, appointments kept, and the full cost of the proposed setup. When sales attribution is available, review sold vehicles too.
