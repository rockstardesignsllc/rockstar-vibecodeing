How fast can my dealership go live?

Most stores launch within days, not months. We build your AI workforce around your inventory feed, your process, and your team, then test it with real dealership questions before it ever talks to a customer.

What does RockstarAI cost?

Custom pricing, built around your store. You only pay for the AI agents you actually need: voice, SMS, web chat, or all three. No bundles, no bloat, no paying for a photography suite you didn't ask for. Book a discovery call and we'll price your exact setup.

Does this replace my BDC or help it?

Your call. Some stores use RockstarAI to cover nights, weekends, and overflow so the BDC focuses on hot leads. Others replace outsourced BDC spend entirely. Either way, every lead gets answered in seconds instead of hours.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

It hands off to your team with full context, so the customer never repeats themselves. Because it's built on your actual inventory and process, "I don't know" happens a lot less than it does with template bots.

Do I own my customer data?

Yes. Your customers, conversations, and leads are your dealership's data. Full stop.
