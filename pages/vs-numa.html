RockstarAI vs Numa for car dealerships.

Both companies offer AI for dealership communication. The products differ in scope, call handling, and configuration. Review what each actually does before your proposal meeting.

About Numa

What Numa offers.

Numa is an AI platform built for automotive retail. Their product focuses on business texting, missed call handling, and customer communication across the dealership. Numa integrates with CRMs and works with both sales and service departments.

Numa's tools include AI-powered text responses, appointment reminders, and call management. Some Numa features include trade valuation-related workflows. Confirm which Numa modules are included in your specific proposal, as product availability can vary by tier and market.

About RockstarAI

What RockstarAI offers.

RockstarAI is an OpenAI-powered AI workforce from Rockstar Designs LLC. It is configured around your dealership's specific inventory, staff, hours, and process.

AI Sales handles shopper questions and appointment booking. AI BDC manages internet lead follow-up. AI Receptionist covers inbound phone calls. Each workflow is tested with your dealership before launch. RockstarAI does not include trade valuation — that remains a separate tool decision for your store.

What to Verify

Compare these details before you decide.

Request this information in writing from any vendor before signing a contract.

Missed call handling

Confirm whether the AI answers missed calls in real time or only sends a follow-up text afterward. Test it with a live call to your proposed number.

Sales call capability

Ask specifically whether the AI handles inbound sales calls live, or whether the phone product is limited to service and missed-call follow-up text responses.

Lead source connections

Identify which lead providers connect to the platform and how quickly the AI responds to a new internet lead after it arrives.

Appointment booking

Confirm whether the AI books appointments directly into your scheduling system or routes to a human. Ask what calendar integrations are supported.

Total cost of ownership

Request the itemized proposal: setup, monthly fees, usage charges, integration costs, and what happens to pricing after the initial contract term.

Powersports support

If you operate a powersports or RV dealership, confirm whether the platform is configured for non-automotive inventory types or automotive-only.

How to Test

Make the comparison specific.

Run the same test with both vendors using your actual leads and phone number. The result tells you more than any demo presentation.

01

Submit an internet lead

Use a real phone number. Ask about a specific vehicle. Note how fast you get a response and whether it answers your actual question.

02

Call after hours

Call both systems at 8pm. Determine whether the AI answers live or whether it fires a text callback. Ask a real sales question and note the response.

03

Evaluate the follow-up

After your test lead, go silent for 48 hours. Watch how each system follows up. Evaluate timing, message quality, and whether it sounds like your store.
