RockstarAI vs Numa for dealership customer conversations

This comparison is published by Rockstar Designs LLC, the provider of RockstarAI. Public product descriptions establish advertised capabilities, not a proven performance winner.

What Numa offers

Numa for Sales advertises inventory conversations, trade valuation, test-drive booking, CRM lead delivery, and routing to sales staff. Numa Operator handles inbound reception and department routing. Numa also describes a shared Sales Inbox.

Sources: Numa for Sales and Numa Operator.

What RockstarAI offers

RockstarAI Sales helps shoppers ask about vehicles and book a test drive. BDC handles connected leads and approved follow-up. Reception answers approved questions, captures messages, and routes calls. Your selected agents are configured around the dealership's information, schedule, and staff responsibilities. RockstarAI captures trade details for your team. Trade values come from your dealership's appraisal process. This is an important distinction to test if automated valuation is part of your Numa proposal.

Why consider RockstarAI

Our starting point is the workload in your store. We identify the communication gaps, build the relevant workflows, and test your inventory questions and handoffs. Evaluate that implementation alongside the Numa setup proposed for your dealership.

Compare these details before deciding

  • What is answered automatically and what is handed to staff?
  • Which inventory source and update schedule will the AI use?
  • Does trade handling collect details, estimate a value, or provide an offer?
  • Where do leads, messages, and conversation notes appear?
  • What happens when a transfer destination does not answer?
  • Which products, usage costs, and integration work are included?

Run the same test

Call about a vehicle, discuss a trade, ask for a test drive, and request a salesperson. Check each step and the information delivered to staff. Repeat after hours.
