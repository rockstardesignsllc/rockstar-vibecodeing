Measuring What Matters

What to track when you add AI to your dealership.

Knowing the right metrics before you start means you can evaluate your AI setup honestly — not just count conversations. Pull your baseline numbers now, then check at 30, 60, and 90 days after go-live.

Lead Response

First response time and quality.

How fast a lead gets a response matters. So does the response itself. Track both, and track what happens to leads that receive a fast response versus those that wait.

  • Average time from lead submission to first response
  • Percentage of leads answered within 5 minutes
  • After-hours leads that received a response vs went unanswered
  • Leads that progressed to appointment after first response
  • Leads that disengaged before getting a response

Phone Coverage

Calls answered and handled.

Missed calls cost deals. Measuring your phone coverage baseline before AI helps you see the actual change after implementation.

  • Total inbound calls vs calls answered
  • After-hours call answer rate before and after
  • Calls that reached a human vs calls that did not
  • Appointments booked over the phone
  • Calls transferred correctly to the right department

Appointments

Booked, confirmed, and kept.

An appointment booked by AI is only valuable if the customer shows up. Track the full chain, not just the booking event.

  • Appointments booked through AI vs through staff
  • Appointments confirmed with the customer before the date
  • Show rate for AI-booked appointments
  • Appointments rescheduled vs cancelled
  • Time between first contact and appointment date

Follow-Up

Contacts that stayed engaged.

Most leads don't buy the first week. Follow-up quality separates stores that convert long-term from those that forget after day three.

  • Leads that received a follow-up within 24 hours
  • Leads that responded to follow-up communication
  • Days between first contact and purchase for AI-touched leads
  • Leads that went dormant vs stayed in active communication

Staff Time

What your team stopped doing.

One of the clearest AI results is what your team no longer has to handle. Track this alongside customer metrics.

01

Routine questions handled by AI vs escalated to staff

02

After-hours contacts handled without staff involvement

03

Time spent by sales staff on repeat follow-up tasks

04

Handoffs where the customer arrived already informed

Before You Go Live

Set your baseline now.

Pull your current numbers for response time, call answer rate, appointment show rate, and follow-up completion before you launch any AI. Those are your baseline. Use them to evaluate 30, 60, and 90 days after go-live.

Results depend on your dealership's specific configuration, traffic volume, CRM setup, and team participation. RockstarAI cannot guarantee outcome numbers before implementation. Your proposal will define the scope being implemented and what metrics your store will track.
