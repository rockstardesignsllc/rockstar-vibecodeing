RockstarAI vs STELLA for car dealerships.

About STELLA

What STELLA offers.

STELLA Automotive AI is built specifically for car dealerships with a focus on voice AI for inbound calls. Their platform uses conversational AI to handle dealership phone calls, route customers, answer common questions, and support both sales and service departments.

STELLA's products are designed around the inbound call experience. Depending on your proposal, their tools may also include outbound follow-up and texting workflows. Review your specific STELLA proposal to confirm which modules and channels are included.

About RockstarAI

What RockstarAI offers.

RockstarAI is an OpenAI-powered AI workforce from Rockstar Designs LLC, configured around your dealership's inventory, staff, hours, and process — not built from a fixed template.

AI Sales handles shopper conversations and appointment booking. AI BDC manages internet lead follow-up. AI Receptionist covers inbound calls and routing. Each workflow is confirmed and tested before launch. RockstarAI also serves powersports dealers: motorcycle, ATV, UTV, RV, and marine.

What to Verify

Compare these details before you decide.

Request this information in writing from any vendor before signing a contract.

Live call handling vs routing

Ask whether the AI handles the full conversation or routes the caller to a department. Test it live with a sales question that requires a specific answer.

After-hours coverage

Call both phone numbers at 8pm on a weeknight with a real question. Note whether the AI answers, what it can do, and whether it books an appointment.

Outbound follow-up

Confirm whether the platform initiates outbound texts or calls to internet leads and how quickly it does so after a new lead arrives.

Text and web chat channels

Verify whether SMS and web chat are included in the proposed package or require a separate module and additional cost.

Dealership-specific configuration

Ask how the platform is configured for your store's inventory, hours, staff names, and approved responses. Confirm what happens when your information changes.

Contract and cancellation

Request the full contract length, cancellation terms, and what happens to your data and phone number if you leave the platform.

How to Test

Make the comparison specific.

Run the same call and lead test on both systems before you sign anything. Real results from your actual phone number and leads tell you more than any demo.

01

Call during business hours

Ask about a specific vehicle in stock. Ask about pricing. Note whether the AI has your actual inventory information or gives a generic response.

02

Call after hours

Call both systems after 8pm. Note what the AI can do, whether it books appointments, and how the conversation ends for the customer.

03

Submit an internet lead

Use a real number and ask about a vehicle. Note the first response time, response quality, and whether the follow-up sequence sounds like your store.
