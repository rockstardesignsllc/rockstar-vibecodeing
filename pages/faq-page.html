Common Questions

RockstarAI FAQ.

Answers to the questions dealers ask most before the AI audit call. If yours isn't here, the audit is the fastest way to get a direct answer for your store.

What does RockstarAI actually do?

RockstarAI is an OpenAI-powered AI workforce configured for your dealership. It handles three jobs: AI Sales (shopper conversations and appointment booking via text and web chat), AI BDC (internet lead follow-up), and AI Receptionist (live inbound phone calls). Each product is configured with your inventory, staff, hours, and approved responses before launch.

How is RockstarAI different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions from a fixed script. RockstarAI uses OpenAI to have real conversations — it understands what a customer is asking, responds with accurate information about your inventory and store, and adapts based on what the customer says next. It handles follow-up sequences, appointment booking, and after-hours phone calls without a human in the loop.

Does RockstarAI answer phone calls live?

Yes. AI Receptionist answers inbound calls to your dealership's AI phone number in real time — days, nights, and weekends. It handles questions about inventory, hours, directions, pricing, and financing basics. It books appointments directly. It transfers to a human when appropriate. You define what that threshold is.

How fast does it respond to internet leads?

AI BDC fires a personalized response within seconds of a new lead arriving — not minutes. The first response is customized to the specific vehicle the customer asked about and the source they came from. Follow-up sequences continue automatically until the customer responds, books an appointment, or opts out.

How long does it take to get up and running?

Most dealerships are live within 5–10 business days of completing onboarding. During that time, we configure your AI with your inventory data, staff information, hours, and process rules. Your team reviews and approves everything before the AI handles its first real conversation. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

Do you work with powersports dealers?

Yes. RockstarAI works with motorcycle, ATV, UTV, side-by-side, RV, and marine dealers — not just car dealers. If your inventory isn't traditional automotive, the AI is configured around your specific product lines and customer questions.

What CRMs and lead sources do you integrate with?

We connect with major automotive CRMs and lead providers. The exact integrations available for your store are confirmed during the audit and outlined in your proposal. Setup fees for integrations are itemized separately so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

The AI is configured to acknowledge when a question is outside its scope and hand off to your team. It doesn't guess or fabricate information. You define what the AI does in edge cases — including which questions always route to a human and how handoff is communicated to the customer.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on which products you implement (AI Sales, AI BDC, AI Receptionist) and what integrations your store requires. Every proposal is itemized — setup, monthly fee, and usage — so you know exactly what you're paying for before you sign. Start with the free AI audit to get a scoped proposal for your store.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. You can call our AI phone demo at 866-521-3366, text the demo number, or submit a test lead at aifordealers.com/choose-your-ai-demo. During the free audit, we also walk you through a live demo configured for your store's inventory and process before you make any commitment.
