Questions about AI for car dealerships

Find answers about RockstarAI, how it works, what it costs, and how to evaluate it for your dealership.

What is RockstarAI?

RockstarAI is an OpenAI-powered AI workforce from Rockstar Designs LLC. It helps dealerships handle customer conversations through configured Sales, BDC, and Reception workflows. AiFordealers.com is the automotive website for demos, comparisons, and a free AI audit.

Is RockstarAI powered by OpenAI?

Yes. RockstarAI uses OpenAI technology as part of its configured dealership workflows. Rockstar Designs LLC provides the implementation and support described in your proposal. OpenAI powering the technology does not imply an OpenAI endorsement.

Can RockstarAI handle sales leads and phone calls?

Yes. The selected Sales, BDC, and Reception workflows can support connected lead conversations and phone handling. Your audit determines the agents, channels, routing, and connections required for your dealership.

Can RockstarAI book appointments?

Appointment booking is available through the configured workflow. We confirm the scheduling connection and test the rules for your dealership before launch.

Can RockstarAI replace our BDC?

It can support selected BDC responsibilities or cover a broader workflow, depending on your setup. Start by defining the tasks, staff handoffs, and results you expect. Evaluate the performance before making staffing changes.

Will RockstarAI work with our CRM?

We confirm compatibility during the audit. The connection method and supported actions depend on your CRM, lead delivery, available access, and proposed configuration.

How long does setup take?

Your implementation timeline depends on the selected agents, required access, integrations, and testing. Your proposal includes the expected schedule and what your dealership needs to provide.

What happens when the customer needs a person?

We configure the handoff rules, destinations, and supported notifications with your team. The evaluation includes requests for a person, unavailable staff, and after-hours situations.

What does RockstarAI cost?

Pricing is based on your dealership's scope. Request an itemized proposal that includes setup, recurring charges, usage, integrations, support, and contract terms. See the pricing page.

How do we compare RockstarAI with another provider?

Test the same calls and leads with each proposed setup. Check answer accuracy, bookings, completed handoffs, appointments kept, and the complete cost. Use the comparison pages to identify the product scope to confirm. We have dedicated pages for RockstarAI vs Impel, RockstarAI vs Numa, and RockstarAI vs STELLA.

Does RockstarAI work for powersports dealerships?

Absolutely. Motorcycle, ATV, UTV, RV, and marine. RockstarAI is configured for the vehicle types and seasonal patterns your store operates around, not just four-door sedans.

What is the Customer Engagement Calculator?

It is a payment tool you embed in your site, send via text follow-up, or link from any landing page. Buyers set their own down payment, loan term, and credit tier and see a monthly payment number they built themselves. When they walk in, the price conversation is already over. See it here.
