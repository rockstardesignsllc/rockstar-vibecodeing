About Impel

What Impel offers.

Impel is an automotive AI platform focused on customer lifecycle engagement. Their product suite includes AI-powered communications for vehicle sales, service, and trade-in. Impel works with franchise and independent dealers and integrates with several major CRMs and inventory systems.

Impel's platform includes tools for personalized outreach, automated follow-up, and vehicle merchandising. Their products cover the pre-purchase, purchase, and post-purchase stages. Review their current product pages to confirm which modules are included in any proposal you receive.

About RockstarAI

What RockstarAI offers.

RockstarAI is an OpenAI-powered AI workforce from Rockstar Designs LLC. It is configured around your dealership's inventory, people, and process — not built from a generic template.

What to Verify

Compare these details before you decide.

Request this information in writing from any vendor before signing a contract.

Phone call coverage

Confirm whether the proposed product answers inbound sales calls, after-hours calls, and overflow. Verify whether this is a live AI voice agent or a routing/recording tool.

Lead response workflow

Identify which lead sources are connected, how fast the first response fires, and what the AI does if the customer doesn't reply to the first message.

Appointment booking

Confirm whether the AI books appointments directly into your calendar or simply collects lead information and routes it to your team. Test it with a real request.

Contract and pricing

Request itemized costs: setup, monthly platform fee, usage charges, and integration costs. Ask for the full contract term and cancellation policy in writing.

CRM and DMS integration

Confirm which CRMs and DMSs are supported, what data flows between systems, and which integrations are included in the base price versus billed separately.

Onboarding and support

Ask how long onboarding takes, what your dealership is responsible for providing, and what the response time is for support issues after launch.

How to Test

Run the same test on both.

Before you sign anything, send the same lead to both systems and call both phone numbers with the same question. Measure the response.

01

Submit a real lead

Use your name and a real phone number. Ask about a specific vehicle in stock. Note how fast you hear back and whether the answer is accurate.

02

Call after hours

Call both stores at 8pm on a weeknight. Note whether the AI answers, what it says, and whether it books an appointment or just takes a message.

03

Don't reply for 48 hours

After your initial contact, go silent. Watch how each system follows up. Count the messages, note the timing, and evaluate whether the tone matches your dealership.
