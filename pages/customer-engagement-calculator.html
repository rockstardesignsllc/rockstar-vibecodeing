How It Works

Three steps.
One shift in power.

The customer does the math themselves — so by the time they walk in, they've already decided yes.

01

Customer sets their target payment

They open the calculator on their phone, on your site, or in an AI text — and drag the sliders to a payment that feels right for their budget. No salesperson pressure. Just them and the numbers.

02

Down payment and term adjust in real time

As they move the down payment up or extend the term, the monthly number shifts instantly. They see the trade-offs themselves. They make the choice themselves. That ownership matters.

03

Your team works from agreement

When the customer arrives, they hand you a number they built. You confirm it, not argue against it. The negotiation is already over — you're just closing paperwork.
